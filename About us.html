About AutoNexa

Our Story

AutoNexa was established in 2026 in Johannesburg, South Africa, with a simple goal: to make buying and selling vehicles easier, safer and more transparent.

We recognised that purchsing a pre-owned vehicle can sometimes consuming. customers often have to compare prices and vehicles options before making an important decision.

AutoNexa was created to provide a more convenient digital experience where customers can explore vehicles, compare their options and make informed own homes.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

AutoNexa's mission is to make buying and selling vehicles in South Africa simpler, safer and more transparent by providing convenient digital automotive marketplace.

Our Vision

Our Vision is to become South Africa's most trusted digital automotive marketplace by creating a transparent, convenient and customer-focused way to buy and sell vehicles online.
